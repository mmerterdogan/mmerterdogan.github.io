001How I work

I trust a result once two different routes agree on it, and I want to understand why they disagree when they don't. On a pressure vessel project I calculated the shell stresses by hand, then built the ANSYS model. The model showed that the flange and head discontinuities, not the shell membrane, set the safety factor: 3.05 locally, against 11.2 from shell theory.

A design is not finished for me until I know how it will be built. In the TEKNOFEST UAV team I worked on a landing gear made of carbon-fibre tubes and 3D-printed joints, and in four internships I saw parts go from the design office to the machine shop and the assembly line.

At FESTO and BEKO I learnt that a problem rarely starts where it shows up. I am now doing a thesis MSc in Mechanical Engineering and looking for a team where I can keep learning by building real things.
